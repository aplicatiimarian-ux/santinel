# SANTINEL Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 18, 2026

## 1. Introduction

SANTINEL ("we" or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information.

## 2. Information We Collect

### Directly Provided:
- Name, email, company affiliation
- Negotiation scenarios and coaching requests
- Feedback and ratings
- Session data and interactions

### Automatically Collected:
- IP address and device information
- Browser type and version
- Pages visited and time spent
- Cookies and tracking data

### Third-Party Data:
- Analytics from Google (if enabled)
- Error tracking from Sentry (if enabled)

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data for:
- Providing coaching services
- Improving our algorithms and recommendations
- Analyzing usage patterns
- Sending service updates
- Detecting fraud or security issues
- Complying with legal obligations

**We do NOT:**
- Sell user data to third parties
- Share coaching content with competitors
- Use data for unrelated marketing

## 4. GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

### Your Rights:
- Right to access your data
- Right to rectification of inaccurate data
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Right to restrict processing
- Right to data portability
- Right to object to processing

**To exercise these rights:**
Email: privacy@santinel.io with your request

### Data Retention:
- Session data: 90 days (unless archived)
- Feedback/coaching: 2 years (for improvement)
- Account data: Until deletion request

## 5. Data Security

We implement:
- End-to-end encryption for sensitive data
- TLS/SSL for data in transit
- Database encryption at rest
- Regular security audits
- Access controls and logging

However, no security is 100% guaranteed. You use the Service at your own risk.

## 6. Third-Party Services

We may use third parties for:
- Cloud hosting (ensuring EU data residency)
- Analytics (with privacy settings)
- Email delivery
- Payment processing

These providers must comply with GDPR and sign Data Processing Agreements (DPAs).

## 7. Children's Privacy

SANTINEL is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we discover such collection, we will delete it immediately.

## 8. Cookie Policy

We use cookies for:
- Session authentication
- User preferences (dark/light mode)
- Analytics (optional)

Users can disable non-essential cookies in browser settings.

## 9. Contact & Data Protection Officer

For privacy questions:
- Email: dpo@santinel.io
- Response time: 14 days

For complaints:
- Contact your local data protection authority
- GDPR Article 77 rights apply

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes are effective upon posting. Continued use means acceptance.
